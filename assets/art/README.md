# Art

Draft art for playtesting, exported from the Art Resources sheet. All images are WebP.

- `tile-*` — the 20 town tiles
- `board` — a full 4×4 town (Harbour Hill)
- `dawn-track` — the 10-night track
- `pawn-*`, `specialist-*` — standees
- `token-*`, `item-*` — round tokens and item tokens
- `card-*` — robber, cop, goal, witness, loyalty and event cards, plus card backs
- `mat-*` — the two team mats
