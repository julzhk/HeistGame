# How to play (summary)

This is a short summary. The [full rulebook](../rules/heist-rulebook-A4.pdf) has every rule, a worked example game and clarifications.

## Setup

1. **Build the town.** The Hideout, Police Station and Hardware Store are always used. Shuffle the rest by type and draw 4 of the 5 targets, 5 of the 6 suppliers and 4 of the 6 specials. Lay out the 16 tiles in a 4×4 grid, with the Hideout and Police Station at least 3 steps apart. Tiles never rotate.
2. **Choose the target.** Keep only the goal cards whose target and all three suppliers are in town. The robbers draw one in secret.
3. **Witness deck.** Take one witness card per target in town. The robbers secretly remove the one for their real target. Every card left names a target that *isn't* it.
4. **Pawns.** Robbers start at the Hideout, cops at the Police Station. Each team shares its 3 pawns among its players.
5. **Clock.** Dawn marker on Night 1. Cops take 2 accusation tokens.

## A night

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Briefing | Cops may accuse (spending a token) or call Internal Affairs. Anyone may talk. |
| 2. Plan | Both teams at once: a face-down destination token per pawn (within 2 tiles), up to 2 decoys, and one face-down action card per pawn. |
| 3. Reveal | Flip everything. Move each pawn to its token. |
| 4. Resolve | Chase → Roadblock & Stakeout → Grab, Search & Scout → Heist! → hand-offs. |
| 5. Dawn | Played cards go to the used piles. Flip an event. Move the dawn marker. Shift change (hands refill) after Nights 3, 6 and 8. |

## Actions

| Robbers | | Cops | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grab** | Take an item at a supplier, face-down | **Search** | Robbers who Grabbed on your tile show what they took |
| **Scout** | Peek at the next event card | **Chase** | Catch robbers on your tile or next to it |
| **Lie low** | Can't be caught tonight | **Roadblock** | Nobody may enter or pass this tile next night |
| **Heist!** | On the target with all 3 items: robbers win | **Stakeout** | Every Grab on this tile is shown |

A caught robber drops a random item face-up into the cops' evidence locker and goes back to the Hideout.

## Targets and what they need

| Target | Items |
|---|---|
| Bank | Drill · Blueprints · Guard Schedule |
| Museum | Glass Cutter · Disguise · Guard Schedule |
| Jewellery Store | Glass Cutter · Getaway Car · Blueprints |
| Casino | Disguise · Uniform · Getaway Car |
| Grand Hotel | Master Key · Uniform · Guard Schedule |

## Winning

- **Robbers:** play Heist! on the target with all 3 items carried by the crew between them.
- **Cops:** a correct, unanimous accusation, or Night 10 ends with no heist. A wrong unanimous accusation moves the dawn marker back one night.
- **Lone Wolf (mode 5):** when the cops win, a robber personally carrying a full set for a *different* target in town gets one solo night to pull it off. If it works, only that player wins.
- **Double agents (mode 4):** win with the team they secretly play for.
