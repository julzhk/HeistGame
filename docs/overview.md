# Overview

> **Heist! · Cops & Robbers** · 2–6 players · ages 12+ · about 60 minutes · team deduction and bluffing

## The pitch

One town. One secret target. Ten nights until dawn.

The robbers know what they're after. The cops don't, and they have to agree on it before the robbers strike. Every shop the gang visits is a clue, every move is planned in secret, and every card you spend leaves a trail.

## The setting

Harbour Hill, a 1940s film-noir city at night: wet streets, sodium streetlamps, a neon diner sign and fog rolling in from the docks. The town is built from **20 square city-block tiles**, and each game uses 16 of them. The crew (Rosa, Benny and Ziggy) hide out in a warehouse. The law (Dot, Ray and Kim) work out of the 14th Precinct.

## The two teams

**The robbers** draw a secret goal card naming one target and the three items it needs. They visit suppliers (the Hardware Store, the Theatre, the Café and so on) to grab those items, then play **Heist!** on the target with all three items held somewhere in the crew. They win instantly.

**The cops** don't know the target, but they know every target's shopping list. They search, stake out, chase and set roadblocks to see what the robbers are collecting. When they think they know, 
every cop secretly names a target. If they all agree and they're right, the cops win. They only get two accusations. If the sun comes up after Hour 10 with no heist, the cops win too.

## What makes it different

- **Open movement, hidden intent.** Pawns walk around the board in plain sight. Face-down destination tokens and blank decoys hide where they're *going*, and face-down action cards hide what they'll *do*.
- **The prep leaks the plan.** Each item fits more than one target, so every grab narrows the field without giving it away.
- **A unanimous accusation.** The cops must agree, which makes every vote tense, and in the Inside Job mode one of them might be a double agent.
- **A new town every game.** Which targets and suppliers make it onto the board decides which jobs are possible.
- **Lone Wolf.** Even when the cops guess right, one robber holding a full set for a *different* target can make a solo run and win alone.

## Five modes

| Mode | What it adds | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Petty Theft | The core race | A 20-minute teaching game |
| 2 · Break-In | Chases, stakeouts, roadblocks, decoys, events | First real game; younger players |
| 3 · The Big Job | Team hands that run out and refill at shift change | The standard game |
| 4 · Inside Job | Secret loyalty, Internal Affairs, Shady Deals | The full game, 4+ players |
| 5 · Lone Wolf | One robber's solo getaway | Add to any mode |

Each mode includes the ones before it and plays as a complete game.
