## Open questions for playtesting

Every number in Draft 1 is a first guess. These are the things to watch at the table.

1. **Item pace.** Can the robbers collect 3 items in 10 nights with only 3 Grab cards per shift, while dodging Chases? Or is it too easy?
2. **Accusation penalty.** Is "the dawn marker moves back one night" enough of a cost for a wrong unanimous accusation?
3. **Town size.** Is a 4×4 town right for 1v1, or should smaller games use 12 tiles?
4. **Double agent odds.** With 2 Double Agent cards among 8 in 3v3, the chance of a mole is high. Paranoid in a fun way, or just frustrating?
5. **Internal Affairs bonuses.** Are an accusation token (cops) and a free item (robbers) balanced?
6. **Lone Wolf frequency.** How often does a robber end up holding a full set for another target? If almost never, should targets share more items?
7. **Specialists.** Is any ability too strong, especially the Dog Handler's long Chase?
8. **Master Key.** It only fits the Grand Hotel, which breaks the "every item fits two targets" rule. Add a second goal card that uses it?
9. **Hideout rule.** Cops can't end a move on the Hideout until a robber is caught, but a Chase from next door can reach it. Intended?
10. **Daily Herald.** A cop camping there could rule out every wrong target in 3 nights. Limit it to one draw per cop per shift?
11. **Item token count.** Are 4 tokens per item enough?

Found an answer, or a new problem? See [Playtesting](playtesting.md).
