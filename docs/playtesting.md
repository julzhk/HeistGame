# Playtesting

Thank you for testing Heist! Here's how to make a test game as useful as possible.

## Before you play

1. Print the [print & play kit](../print-and-play/heist-print-and-play-A4.pdf) on A4 card, double-sided, flipping on the long edge. Cut out the tiles, cards and tokens, and fold the standees.
2. Read the [rulebook](../rules/heist-rulebook-A4.pdf). For a first game, play **Break-In** (mode 2).
3. Have someone keep notes: a phone timer and a scrap of paper is enough.

## What to note

- Player count, team split and mode played.
- How long setup and the game took.
- Who won, how, and on which night.
- How many items the robbers had collected by Nights 3, 6 and 8.
- Each accusation: which night, what each cop voted, and whether it was right.
- Any moment where people argued about a rule, or where someone was bored or confused.
- Anything from the [open questions](roadmap.md#open-questions-for-playtesting).
- The best moment of the game. (There should be one!)

## Sending a report

Open a new issue on this repository using the **Playtest report** template, or send your notes to the designer directly.
