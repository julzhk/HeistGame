## Inspiration

The rulebook cover's layout is inspired by a 1960s film poster. All figures on it are the game's own characters.
Heist! sits in a family of great deduction and hidden-role games, including Scotland Yard, Letters from Whitechapel, Burgle Bros and The Resistance.
